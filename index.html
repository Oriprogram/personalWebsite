[autopot.jpg] ESP32 · Soil Sensor · Pump Relay

AutoPot

Ultimate smart planter.

Shipped

Autonomous smart planter built around an ESP32 board-screen hybrid. Coded in C++ via Arduino IDE. Syncs telemetry to Google Firebase over Wi-Fi, allowing remote soil moisture tracking and pump threshold adjustments from a web dashboard. Features reactive Tamagotchi-style screen states based on soil thirst and touch input.

[hydro-cleaner.jpg] Solar Array · EV3 · 4 Motors

HydroEcoCleaner

Ocean Cleanup Intercepter inspired project.

Prototype

Autonomous cleaning boat built to extract floating debris from water surfaces. Handled all hardware: wired an external solar panel to the central EV3 controller, balanced twin-hull weight distribution, and integrated 4 motors (2 paddle wheels for water propulsion, 2 driving an angled conveyor belt into a removable bin).
